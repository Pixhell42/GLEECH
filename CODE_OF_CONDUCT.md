# Community conduct

GLEECH welcomes artists, developers and curious experimenters. Be respectful,
constructive and specific when discussing artwork, bugs and proposals.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, spam and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's
private files or credentials. Share art and presets you have permission to share,
retain appropriate credits and respect channel rules.

Maintainers/moderators may remove content, warn participants or restrict access
to protect the community. If a moderator is involved in a concern, contact
another moderator privately. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

These expectations apply to the repository, issues/PRs and the
[Discord Community](https://discord.gg/b7Mq8fjvk).
